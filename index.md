## Research


<details style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
<summary> <strong> Characterising canonical lifts of ordinary Calabi–Yau varieties </strong> (Oct. '26). <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35807">arXiv.</a>
</summary>
We study Brantner–Taelman's canonical lifts of ordinary Calabi–Yau varieties and characterise their (quasi)canonical lifts in terms of rational \(p\)-adic Hodge theoretic data. We also show that these canonical lifts need not be of CM type and that the associated local Galois representations need not split as sums of twists of unramified representations, in contrast to classical results for abelian varieties and K3 surfaces.</details>


<details style="margin-bottom: 15px;">
<summary> <strong> The period map from commutative to noncommutative deformations </strong> (Dec. '25). <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24347">arXiv.</a> <i> Submitted.</i>
</summary>
We study the period map from infinitesimal deformations of a qcqs derived scheme \(X\) over a field \(k\) to those of the associated \(k\)-linear \(\infty\)-category \(\mathrm{QC}(X)\). We identify the corresponding map on tangent fibres with the dual HKR map \(\mathrm{R}\Gamma(X,\mathbf T_{X/k})[1] \longrightarrow \mathrm{HH}_{\bullet}(X/k)[2]\) and study its injectivity on homotopy groups. As applications, we show liftability of qcqs schemes along square-zero extensions to be a derived invariant, at least when \(\mathrm{char}(k) \ne 2\), and exhibit cases where the entire classical deformation functor of \(X\) is a derived invariant; this partially answers a question of Lieblich.</details>
